## Key Achievements

### AI Strategy Development

Worked closely with the founding team to build an AI roadmap grounded in real patient experience problems rather than generic AI hype:

- Assessed existing product, data, and workflows to identify where AI could meaningfully improve the patient experience
- Prioritized opportunities based on patient impact, feasibility, and time to value
- Defined a phased implementation plan aligned with the startup's resources and stage

### Hands-On Implementation

Moved beyond strategy documents into shipped functionality:

- Designed and built AI-powered features supporting the patient experience
- Integrated AI capabilities into the existing product architecture
- Established patterns for evaluating and iterating on AI feature quality

### Communications & Infrastructure Integration

Connected AI features into the startup's broader product and cloud environment:

- Integrated the Dialpad API to bring AI capabilities into patient communication workflows
- Built serverless processing on AWS Lambda to support AI-driven functionality
- Designed the integration to scale with patient volume without added operational overhead

## Impact

The engagement gave the startup a clear, actionable AI strategy along with working implementation, enabling the team to:

- Move quickly from AI concept to production feature
- Ground AI investment in concrete patient experience outcomes
- Build internal confidence and momentum for future AI-driven product work
