## Key Achievements

### Page Through Time, Stay in Place

- Nine USGS map editions of the Auburn area, from 1891 to 2021, including a 1975 aerial
- Map position and zoom stay fixed as you move between editions
- Each edition is tiled only to its native zoom, so nothing is upscaled past what the source supports

### Honest Sources

Every edition has a source card showing:

- The sheet and scale
- Every recorded date: publication, revision, photography, and field check
- A link to the original map

## Impact

The browser shows what the maps actually recorded and when, rather than reconstructing trail history. It is the first step toward a Nevada and Placer County trail atlas through time.
